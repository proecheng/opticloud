# Team+ Legal Inquiry FAQ

## Who can use the legal inquiry SLA?

Active Team and Enterprise plan users can submit legal inquiries through Console. Free, Starter, Pro, canceled, expired, or period-expired subscriptions do not receive the Team+ legal inquiry SLA.

## What does the 24h SLA mean?

The 24h SLA means the first human response from Customer Success or the legal handoff owner is due within 24 hours of submission. It does not mean a final legal opinion, contract approval, signed DPA, procurement completion, or certification evidence is completed within 24 hours.

## What topics are in scope?

Typical topics include PIPL, GDPR, graded protection, data export, DPA review, open-source or commercial license questions, security review, and buyer procurement questions.

## What happens after submission?

OptiCloud creates a durable internal inquiry record and a Linear-ready ticket reference. The current implementation does not call the external Linear API and does not claim that an external Linear issue was created.

## Is this legal advice?

No. Product responses and FAQ content help route questions to the right owner. Final legal advice, contract text, or signing decisions require the legal process referenced in `docs/legal-templates.md`.
